# Privacy policy

Limit Lines is a free Claude Code plugin made by jafdev. This policy covers what it does with information. Last updated 8 November 2026.

## What it reads

Only the usage figures Claude Code already has for the session it runs in:

- how many tokens the conversation holds, and the window it compacts at
- your 5-hour, weekly and any other usage limits, as a percent used and the time each resets
- the session's cost, where Claude Code reports one

It reads no names, emails, files, messages, passwords, keys or other credentials.

## What it does with them

It draws them as lines above the prompt or in its side pane, on your own screen. That is all.

## What it keeps

The latest figures and your chosen layout (above the prompt, side pane or off) stay in that session's own state on your computer. Limit Lines writes no files and keeps nothing for other sessions.

## What it sends

Nothing. Limit Lines makes no network requests, has no server, uses no analytics or tracking, and shares nothing with anyone, jafdev included.

## Children

Limit Lines is a developer tool and is not made for children.

## Changes and contact

Any change to this policy is made in this file, and its history is public on GitHub. Questions go to [GitHub issues](https://github.com/JAFDevs/claude-usage-bars/issues).
